Discipline & Philosophy

Reduction to the structural essence.

I am Tessal, an Electrical and Computer Engineer. I believe the highest form of engineering craftsmanship is subtractive: removing noise, unnecessary abstractions, and brittle complexity until only the pure, functioning system remains.

From derivation of closed-form Jacobian equations to bare-metal register configuration, I approach systems engineering with patience, precision, and reverence for fundamental principles.
